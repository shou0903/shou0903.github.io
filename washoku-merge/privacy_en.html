Privacy Policy

Effective: September 27, 2026

This policy explains how Washoku Merge ("the app") handles information.

1. Information the developer collects
The developer does not collect your name, email address or anything else that identifies you. There are no accounts.

2. Information kept only on your device
Best scores, the food book, settings and games in progress are stored only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the app.

3. Ads
The app uses Google AdMob by Google LLC to show ads (banners and ads between games are not shown if you buy "Remove Ads" or the "Premium Pass"). AdMob may collect the following to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud:
- The advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking in iOS
- IP address, device type, OS version and approximate location (country or region)
- How often ads are shown and tapped
This is handled under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can change tracking permission at any time in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent to personalized ads as required by law.

4. Apple services
- Game Center: if you are signed in, your scores and achievements are sent to Apple's Game Center and shown on leaderboards. The friends ranking shows your Game Center friends' names, photos and scores (only if you allow access to your friends list).
- In-app purchases: payments are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your card details.

5. Sharing
When you share a result, the picture and text go to the app you choose (such as a social network).

6. Children
The app is suitable for all ages. Ads are limited to content suitable for all ages.

7. Changes
If this policy changes, the app will let you know.
