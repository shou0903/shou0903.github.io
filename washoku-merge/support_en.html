FAQ

My purchase isn't showing
Try Settings > Restore Purchases in the app.

How do I cancel the Premium Pass?
In iPhone Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Rewards you claimed stay yours.

How do I reset my records?
Settings > Erase all records in the app (purchases are not affected).

Friends don't show in the ranking
Sign in to Game Center and allow access to your friends list.
